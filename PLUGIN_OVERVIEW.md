Bring a little terminal character to BB.

Herdr Theme combines eleven light and dark color families with framed conversations,
full-width transcripts, highlighted user messages, compact prompts, and terminal
connectors for subthreads. JetBrains Mono is bundled for offline use.

Choose a color scheme in Settings → Herdr Theme or Settings → Appearance.
BB keeps its native mobile sidebar, editor, attachments, voice controls, model
picker, and keyboard shortcuts. Switching to another theme removes the extra styling.

Inspired by Herdr. An independent community project. Some palettes have light
companions adapted for BB, identified in the color picker.
